Clinical prediction model

PainPredict-TACE

Preprocedural probability of NRS ≥ 4 at 24 hours after transarterial chemoembolization.

Research use only. This model was developed in one centre and assessed by repeated internal validation. It has not been externally validated or tested prospectively, and it must not be used alone to make clinical decisions.

Calculation

The page loads the published model card and applies its raw-scale equation. A laboratory value is capped at the development bounds. If it is missing, the development median is substituted and the missing-indicator coefficient is added.

Probability = 1 / (1 + exp(−linear predictor)).

Limitations

  • Developed and internally validated in one centre; performance elsewhere is unknown.
  • No temporal, external or prospective impact evaluation has been performed.
  • The coefficients are predictive associations, not causal effects.
  • The 0.20 operating point is descriptive and is not a recommended treatment threshold.
  • Preprocedural NRS in the development cohort was 0–3. Values beyond a capping bound contribute only the bound’s effect.

Calculation runs entirely in this browser. Predictor values are neither uploaded nor stored. This browser saves only the selected language.
